How it works · all Stellar TESTNET

Five moves, one tap.

1Passkey → OpenZeppelin smart account. The relay pays the fees.
2A 1,000 USDC per-transaction limit, written on-chain at the first withdrawal.
3The anchor gives an IBAN over SEP-6. Lira in, USDC out.
4A temporary account forwards the USDC to your kumbara and closes itself.
5One approval puts it in the DeFindex vault. Withdrawal runs backwards.
payment 778215b3…forward f580e030…close 6223c61c…

The anchor can only pay a normal Stellar account, not a smart account.

So we create a temporary account whose only two possible transactions are signed before any money arrives: forward the USDC to your kumbara, then close itself.

Nobody, including us, can ever redirect it.

No custody, no trust in us.

Regulatory posture · where it launches

Non-custodial software in front of a licensed anchor.

  • Kumbara never holds lira, USDC or keys.
  • The anchor holds the fiat, does KYC, pays your own IBAN.
  • Withdraw to your own bank or your own address. No merchant flow.
  • On-chain per-transaction cap; anchor-side limits.
  • Gate: the anchor's written position on a non-custodial front end, Tranche 1.
Türkiye first, by fit

31.5% inflation, a transfer habit everyone has, the TRY sandbox this deck runs on.

Fallback built in

Tranche 1 ranks the anchors on the SCF list by market. If Türkiye fails, the pilot launches in the top-ranked market. The app does not change. Market two is the follow-on award.
