# Kumbara v0.2.0-testnet

Released 9 November 2026 on Stellar **testnet**, at https://kumbara.sembol.xyz. Test money only: nothing in this release touches real lira or real dollars.

This is the first reviewed release after the hackathon prototype. It is safer, anyone can now fund their own kumbara, and problems get noticed.

## What's new for people trying Kumbara

- **Fund your kumbara yourself.** A new, empty kumbara shows a "Test money" card. One tap sends 10 test USDC to it in about 15 seconds, and one more approval puts the money in the vault. Nobody has to play the bank for you any more. Each kumbara can do this once. The card says plainly that this is test money with no value.
- **Report a problem in one tap.** Every error screen has a "Report a problem" button that sends what went wrong (the error, the page, your browser type, nothing that identifies you). The footer has the same button with room for a note.
- **See whether it's you or us.** The footer shows a status line that is green when everything works and names the part that is slow or down (anchor, relay, Stellar network, vault).
- **Clearer errors.** When the relay is down you now see "The relay is not answering. Try again", not an unrelated "Vercel login" screen.
- **Renaming a kumbara or cancelling a deposit asks for your passkey.** One extra approval, because these changes are now signed (see Security).

## Security

A full review of the bridge account, the relay, the admin endpoints and the recovery registry is in [`SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md). Six findings were fixed: one high, four medium, one low. Three low-risk items are written down as accepted for testnet. The headline fixes:

- Rate limits can no longer be bypassed with a forged IP header.
- The relay only pays fees for Kumbara's own accounts.
- Hashes the browser reports are checked on chain before the server believes them.
- Changing someone's kumbara needs a signature from that kumbara's passkey, not just knowledge of public data.
- A backup passkey is trusted only once the chain shows it as a signer.

## Reliability

- Testnet's upgrade to protocol 30 broke ledger reads. The SDK is updated.
- The hackathon's mock anchor disappeared, so deposits now fall back to `testanchor.stellar.org` automatically.
- Drills, repeatable with one command each:
  - `pnpm drill:recovery`: a backup passkey reopens the same kumbara on a fresh device.
  - `pnpm drill:failures`: a cancelled passkey prompt, a relay outage and a slow then dropped connection, each with a working recovery path.

## For the presenter

- `/booth/admin/funds`: the test-funds pool's balance, grants per day, refusals, and a one-step refill.
- `/booth/admin/monitor`: errors grouped by kind and page, errors per day, and every problem report.

## Not in this release

- **Withdrawing to a bank account (IBAN)** is unavailable until a lira anchor is connected (planned for SOW 2). The previous mock anchor no longer exists.
- No mainnet, no real money, no third-party audit.
