Problem

Lira savings lose value; the dollar route has a wall in front of it.

Inflation makes lira saving a losing move

Consumer prices in Türkiye rose 31.51% year on year in August 2026.1 Dollar-denominated saving is how ordinary households defend themselves.

The crypto route exists but sheds users at every step

USDC on Stellar settles in seconds for fractions of a cent. Getting there means an extension or app install, twelve words to keep safe, XLM for gas bought somewhere else, and a phone you must never lose.

The people who need hard-currency savings most are served least

A bank-transfer habit and a phone with Face ID are what people have. That is the whole interface Kumbara asks for: no seed phrase, no XLM, no install.

How it works

Seven steps, one flow: anchor in front, DeFindex behind, a fee-sponsoring relay underneath.

1The visitor opens an OpenZeppelin smart account with Face ID; the relay pays the fees.
2A spending limit (1,000 USDC per transaction) is written on-chain in the background.
3The anchor issues an IBAN and a reference; the visitor sends lira (simulated by the server on testnet).
4The anchor converts the lira to USDC and pays an ownerless, pre-authorized bridge account.
5The bridge account forwards the USDC to the kumbara and closes itself; nobody holds its key.
6One Face ID puts the USDC into the DeFindex vault (no yield on testnet).
7A withdrawal runs the path backwards: vault → bridge account → anchor → lira to the IBAN.

The bridge · what we found and what we built

Anchors pay classic accounts. Smart accounts are contracts. Ramp payouts could not reach them.

The finding

The anchor's on-ramp rejects a contract (C…) destination and its watcher matches classic payments only: a payout cannot land in a smart account, and a smart account's Soroban transfer is never matched on the way back.

The solution: ownerless, pre-authorized bridge accounts

A throwaway classic account whose only valid transactions are signed before it exists: the forward at sequence +1 (USDC to the user's smart account, the exact quoted amount) and the cleanup at sequence +2 (trustline dropped, reserves back to the sponsor). The master key is wiped in-process: no key can sign anything else. In reverse, the same primitive carries withdrawals to the anchor's treasury with the memo id.

Regulatory positioning

Non-custodial software in front of a licensed anchor.

  • Kumbara never holds lira, USDC or keys. Every transfer is authorized by the visitor's own passkey on their own smart account; the bridge accounts have no owner.
  • The licensed anchor is the regulated party: it holds the fiat, performs KYC and pays out only to the customer's own named IBAN.
  • No payments feature in Türkiye: no send, no P2P, no merchant flow. Deposit into a vault and withdraw back to your own IBAN, nothing else.
  • Turkish AML rules (MASAK) shape the UX: named-IBAN in and out, an on-chain per-transaction cap, anchor-side limits and no cash or third-party paths.
  • A Turkish regulatory opinion is budgeted in Tranche 1, before any mainnet pilot; the mainnet path stays behind a capped, presenter-only flag until then.
